# Infosys Specialist Programmer L3 (₹21 LPA) — DSA Interview Checklist

> **Goal:** Prepare specifically for the Infosys Specialist Programmer L3 interview/coding rounds.
>
> **Strategy:** Do not try to solve 500 random LeetCode problems. Finish the **Core + High Priority** problems below, then use the Standard/Advanced section to close gaps.
>
> Recent candidate reports show that L3 coding can include medium/hard DSA, with **graphs, DP, trees, greedy, sliding window, linked lists, advanced arrays, and advanced data structures** appearing across different interviews/assessments. The exact questions vary by panel and batch, so this is a high-coverage preparation list rather than a guaranteed question bank.
